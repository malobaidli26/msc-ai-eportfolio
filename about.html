About Me

I am Mariam Al Obaidly, a postgraduate student on the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme at the University of Essex Online. This e-Portfolio documents my learning, the artefacts I have produced and my reflections as I progress through the programme.

Professional background

I have a background in computer engineering and around 15 years of experience as a network engineer. I currently work as a senior network engineer in the broadcast media sector in Doha, Qatar, where my work covers enterprise network infrastructure, data centre switching and network security.

My professional interests include network security, automation and governance. I joined the MSc to understand how data analysis and Artificial Intelligence can support better decisions in technical environments such as network operations.
