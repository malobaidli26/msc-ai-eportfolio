Module 2

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Module Overview

The Understanding Artificial Intelligence module explores the foundations, applications, development processes, and professional responsibilities associated with Artificial Intelligence. It covers ethical and governance considerations, machine learning and deep learning approaches, feature engineering, supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and the future of AI systems.

Overall Module Reflection

This module significantly expanded my understanding of Artificial Intelligence from both theoretical and practical perspectives. I progressed from learning the fundamental concepts and ethical principles of AI to applying machine learning techniques using practical tools such as Jupyter Notebook, Scikit-learn and PyTorch. Throughout the module, I developed an appreciation of the complete AI development lifecycle, including data preparation, feature engineering, supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and responsible AI governance. Overall, this module strengthened both my technical knowledge and my ability to critically evaluate AI systems within real-world organisational contexts.
